Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Taqwa respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed and how. 1. WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES Your prayer history, settings, goals, mood records, tasbih statistics and onboarding answers (including name, email, age range) are stored ONLY on your device. Taqwa has no server of its own; this data is never sent to us or to third parties. 2. LOCATION Your location is used to calculate prayer times and show the Qibla direction. Only latitude/longitude are sent to the prayer times service (aladhan.com); the result is cached on your device, never stored by us and NEVER sold. 3. ANALYTICS & CRASH REPORTS We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the app. They collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g. which screen was opened) and crash logs. This data is not matched to your identity and is not used for advertising. 4. FEEDBACK If you send feedback, your message, your name and email (if provided) are forwarded to the developer via an email service (EmailJS), solely so we can reply to you. 5. PURCHASES Subscription payments are processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play; we never see your payment details. Subscription status is verified via RevenueCat. 6. ADS & DATA SELLING Taqwa contains no ads. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing purposes. 7. DELETING YOUR DATA Settings → Account → "Reset profile" permanently deletes all Taqwa data on your device. 8. CONTACT Questions: y.s.akyurek21@gmail.com
